〰

Air

Often associated with mobility and evasion. Practice movement and learn each ability’s range and cooldown.

Guilds guide

Guilds: everything in one place

Guilds are player groups for teaming up and building a community. BendersMC has shared a guild tutorial, but a complete official command list was not publicly verifiable when this page was updated. Use the in-game guild menu/help to confirm exact syntax.

🏰

Find the guild menu

Try the main /guild command if it is enabled on your current BendersMC mode. Check the menu for available actions and follow its prompts.

🤝

Join or invite friends

Look for options such as invites, members or recruitment in the guild menu. Ask the guild owner or staff how invites are accepted on the current version.

🛡️

Roles & permissions

Guild systems commonly have an owner and member roles. Only give leadership or moderation permissions to people you trust. Check the menu to see which roles BendersMC currently supports.

💬

Guild chat

Look for a guild-chat option in the menu or official help. Keep private details, passwords and account information out of public and guild chat.

🏡

Base, claims & safety

Do not assume a guild automatically protects land. BendersMC Survival separately documents land claims using a golden shovel; check the rules for the mode you play.

Official Survival guide ↗
📜

Rules & disputes

Before starting a guild, agree on respectful behaviour, who manages shared resources, and what happens if someone leaves. Ask staff to settle disputes rather than retaliating.

Guild setup checklist

Before you start

Use this checklist to organise your group without guessing how the plugin works.

1. Pick a name

Choose a unique, appropriate guild name that follows server rules.

2. Decide leadership

Choose a reliable owner and agree who may invite or remove members.

3. Confirm features

Ask whether guild chat, guild homes, land protection, banks, alliances or wars are available. These are not guaranteed features.

4. Set expectations

Agree on shared builds, items, contributions and how members resolve disagreements.

5. Keep the group safe

Never share passwords or one-time login codes. Be cautious of links and trades from strangers.

6. Check official help

BendersMC's official pages and Discord are the best places to confirm current guild behaviour and commands.
